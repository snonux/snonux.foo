Deco theme fonts
================

This directory bundles the web fonts:

    limelight-v21-latin-400.woff2     (Limelight, weight 400)
    poiret-one-v18-latin-400.woff2    (Poiret One, weight 400)
    josefin-sans-v34-latin-var.woff2  (Josefin Sans, variable weight 400-600)

Subset: latin.
