Architects Daughter and IBM Plex Mono
=====================================

This directory bundles the web fonts:

    architects-daughter-v20-latin-400.woff2   (weight 400)
    ibm-plex-mono-v20-latin-400.woff2         (weight 400)
    ibm-plex-mono-v20-latin-600.woff2         (weight 600)

Subset: latin.
